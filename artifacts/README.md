# Artifact scope

The current service revision is bound by `validation/engineering-review-manifest.json`. Its named current reports, saved board, source, manufacturingZIP/tables, assemblyPDF, A4/browser and mountedmechanical files validate their specific offline scopes.

Other existing reports/routes/publicationreceipts are historical development evidence. A previous PASS or publicationversion does not validate this revision. Earlier review prose is explicitly copied under `historical-review-2026-10-08/`. Original routerSES/DSN/autorouteroutputs are provenance, not a claim that the new service revision exactly replays obsolete copper modifications. Accepted final routing is the saved board/rootJSON, with source-net invariance and current DRC/CAM checked separately.

No hardware measurements, supplier placement approval, materialcertificates, filled/capped-via qualification or productionrelease is implied.
