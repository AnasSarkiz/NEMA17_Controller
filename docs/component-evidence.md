# Component evidence review — 2026-10-08

This review separates manufacturer specifications, cached supplier metadata, and unverified assumptions. It records findings observed before the design corrections in this review; see the engineering decision record and current connectivity checks for their disposition. It does not approve fabrication or verify live stock.

## Findings requiring correction or qualification

1. **CH224K VBUS sensing:** WCH CH224DS1 version 1F, sections 4.3/7.2, specifies a series resistor for physical pin 8 and a 13.5 V absolute limit at this pin. A direct connection to the 15 V motor bus violates this rating. Section 5.5 permits pin 8 to be unconnected for PD-only operation, while chip DP/DM must be shorted and disconnected from connector data. The manufacturer page 5 reference example uses a 10 kΩ series resistor where voltage sensing is retained. The separate VDD pin requires the 1 kΩ feed and 1 µF bypass.
2. **A4988 safe defaults:** Allegro 4988-DS Rev. 5 specifies ±20 µA logic-input leakage and VIH/VIL of 0.7/0.3 × VDD. A 100 kΩ enable pull-up or sleep pull-down permits a 2 V offset at maximum leakage; the intended safe state is not guaranteed at 3.3 V. A 10 kΩ pull permits 0.2 V offset and has sufficient static margin. Reset and firmware must also respect these polarity defaults.
3. **A4988 current accuracy:** The ±5% trip error is specified at **VREF = 2 V**, DAC 70.71% or 100%; the 38.27% DAC point permits ±15%. There is no specified maximum error at the existing approximately 0.6735 V VREF. Consequently, 0.3508 A is a nominal formula result, not a qualified maximum current. The reference input also permits ±3 µA: a 39 kΩ/10 kΩ divider has 7.96 kΩ Thévenin resistance and up to 23.88 mV reference shift. Reducing both resistors by ten preserves the setpoint while reducing this shift to 2.39 mV. Neither this improvement nor an assumed ±5% allowance qualifies all motor currents; phase-current measurements remain required.
4. **CH32 ADC drive, original circuit:** The original direct 100 kΩ/10 kΩ bus divider had 9.09 kΩ source impedance. The corrected switched output has approximately 4.76 kΩ; see the implemented isolation section below. WCH V2.3 Table 3-24 permits only 6.14 kΩ at 8 MHz, 11-cycle sampling. Use a slower ADC clock and adequate acquisition/filter-settling time. The 10 nF reservoir gives nominal 90.9 µs RC time constant; wait approximately 0.5 ms after a bus transition for initial settling, with measured sampling-rate validation. ADC channel 3 on PA3 is unavailable in certain lots (fifth-to-last lot digit zero), so host detection on PA3 should use a digital input.
5. **Exact component ratings:** Cached supplier records identify C13585 as a 50 V input capacitor, C45783 as 25 V output capacitors, and C1525 as 16 V bypass/bootstrap capacitors. Earlier source annotations of 35 V/10 V/35 V do not describe these exact MPNs. The bootstrap sees BST-to-SW differential voltage, not the entire bus; the AP63203 absolute BST differential limit is 6 V. Voltage rating alone does not establish effective capacitance under DC bias.

6. **Bulk ripple qualification and replacement:** The original C72522 RVT1V470M0605 was 47 µF / 35 V with cached supplier ripple metadata of only 54 mA at 120 Hz and no verified primary ESR/thermal data. It is replaced with **native JLCPCB C178585 Panasonic EEEFPV101XAP, 100 µF ±20%, 35 V**, using the manufacturer exact ordering row. The FP specification rates **0.60 Arms at 100 kHz / 105 °C**, 0.57 Arms at 10 kHz and 0.39 Arms at 120 Hz; ESR is ≤0.16 Ω at 100 kHz / 20 °C. Native pad geometry and polarity are identical to the removed part. The manufacturer specifies 6.3 × 7.7 ±0.3 mm; the native supplier STEP/OBJ measures about 5.82 mm high and is a generic short-case model. Native geometry is retained; mechanical review separately checks the manufacturer maximum envelope with explicit USER_REVIEW. Actual mixed-frequency ripple, motor-heated temperature, reflow and service life remain prototype tests.

## Power and layout implications

The AP63203 Table 2 reference design uses 3.9 µH, 10 µF input, two 22 µF output and 100 nF bootstrap. Its general guidance permits 2.2–10 µH; 4.7 µH is within that range. Ideal CCM ripple at 15 V to 3.3 V, 4.7 µH and 1.1 MHz is approximately 0.498 A peak-to-peak. The 0.15 A screening load is in PFM/DCM, so the CCM peak/RMS estimates are not predictions of actual light-load current. Exact SRN4018 saturation, RMS and DCR limits remain unverified.

AP63203 layout guidance requires the input capacitor close to VIN/GND, close feedback sensing, capacitor ground vias and a ground layer below the IC. A4988 requires a heavy ground plane, an exposed-pad star ground, short sense traces and low-impedance returns directly to that star point. All bypass values and pin maps must be checked on the final netlist; connectivity alone does not establish sufficiently short power loops.

For the original HT7533 circuit, 40–70 mA were conservative load-screening scenarios, **not typical CH32 consumption measurements**. At 14.6 V input these imply 0.452–0.791 W, but WCH typical MCU current plus Allegro maximum driver logic current is approximately 12.2 mA before USB/IO/other loads. Exact Holtek voltage, thermal, tolerance and stability data could not be verified; any decision to retain or replace it must preserve that distinction.

## Unresolved sourcing and prototype evidence

- **SMF18A C19077512:** exact selected manufacturer identity, clamp voltage/current and pulse/repetitive-energy derating remain unverified. Generic Vishay data is not evidence for another selected manufacturer. An 18 V TVS name does not mean an 18 V clamp. Measure VM regeneration/hot-plug overshoot with the selected part.
- **SRN4018-4R7M C780206:** obtain the exact Bourns table and check saturation criterion, RMS criterion, DCR and temperature derating against measured converter peaks.
- **USBLC6-2SC6 C2687116:** verify exact selected manufacturer and primary capacitance/clamp ratings. Library pin mapping is secondary evidence; system ESD performance remains untested.
- **Capacitors and sense resistors:** obtain exact manufacturer DC-bias, tolerance/temperature, ESR/ripple and derating evidence. Cached nominal supplier ratings do not verify live stock or purchasing acceptability.
- **Regeneration:** winding inductive energy is 0.5 × 24 mH × I² per phase, but mechanical energy returned during deceleration can be larger. Motor-load inertia/speed are not specified, so a TVS energy qualification cannot be inferred from winding energy alone.

Manufacturing release remains blocked until the outstanding evidence and prototype tests are resolved. The explicitly authorized native bulk-capacitor substitution is recorded above and in both supplier catalogs. No publication or component order was performed.

## Implemented MCU input isolation

Current sources replace direct divider-fed host detection with native Diodes C94514 MMBT3904-7-F, a 10 kΩ base feed and 10 kΩ collector pull to V3V3; DATA_PRESENT is **active low**. VM uses exact native TI C131992 SN74CBTLV1G125DCKR: 100 kΩ/10 kΩ input divider, isolated output with 10 kΩ bleed and 10 nF reservoir, 100 nF switch bypass, and 10 kΩ OE pull to V3V3. [TI SCDS057H](../references/SN74CBTLV1G125-SCDS057H.pdf) verifies Ioff ≤10 µA at VCC=0 with either port 0–3.6 V; output bleed gives ≤0.101 V DC at its 1% high corner. It explicitly recommends the OE pull-up for power up/down high impedance; a spare MCU GPIO asserts LOW only after logic-rail validation. ON-state divider factor is 21 and source impedance approximately 4.76 kΩ. Physical rail ramps, fast shutdown/output-capacitor decay, NPN collector voltage when unpowered and firmware remain validation gates. Original direct-input defect evidence is retained separately; no claim of bench qualification is made.
