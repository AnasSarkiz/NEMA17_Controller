# CH32X035 schematic verification — v1.6.0

Five functional A4 sheets replace the former per-component pages: Power & USB-PD; Programming & USB; Controller & clocks; Stepper motor driver; Temperature & feedback. Local circuits use real orthogonal wires. Power rails and named interfaces link sheets. Unused physical pins have explicit NC crosses. Same-net branches have junction dots; different-net transverse crossings use native bridge marks. Resistors, capacitors, crystal, diodes and transistor pins use electrical symbols with preserved physical pin identities; the RGB symbol shows three dies in one common-anode package.

The structure follows Rishabh’s [RP2040 reference](https://tscircuit.com/imrishabh18/rp2040-motor-controller#schematic). The reference was inspected as Circuit JSON and rendered, rather than copied from its screenshot. The electrical design remains specific to this controller.

Native `tsci check schematic-placement` reports **zero issues**. The independent [analysis](../artifacts/schematic-redesign/analysis.json) checks **63 components, 241 physical pins, 50 connected source nets and 26 explicitly unused pins**. It verifies conductor geometry and label anchors, complete geometric connectivity through named labels, no different-net wire contacts or overlaps, explicit crossings/junctions, physical pin coverage and A4 bounds. All five native renderings were visually reviewed for readable symbols, values, notes and functional grouping. [Vector PDF](../artifacts/schematic-a4.pdf).

The [preservation receipt](../artifacts/schematic-redesign/preservation.json) records the original v1.6.0 schematic-only change. In v1.7.0, resistor placements and affected copper/Gerbers intentionally change. All source components, physical pins, net assignments and the five qualified schematic sheets remain identical to the v1.6.0 baseline. See [resistor edge clearance](resistor-edge-clearance.md) and its actual Gerber/pad audit for current hardware evidence.

The full board qualification is recorded in [current verification](mounting-usb-verification.md). Assembly BOM/CPL, paste/stencil and silkscreen findings remain manufacturing blockers; hardware/firmware/bench validation and rear motor compatibility remain unverified. Passing schematic analysis is not evidence that assembled hardware has been tested.
